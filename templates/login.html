About the System

CCS Sit-in Monitoring System

The CCS Sit-in Monitoring System is designed to help monitor and manage student sit-in sessions, reservations, evaluations, and laboratory activities across the entire campus in a more organized and efficient way.

The system allows students from different colleges and departments within the campus to participate in laboratory sit-ins, reserve available computer laboratories, monitor remaining sessions, and track participation records in real-time.

🧑‍💻

Student Monitoring

Track active and completed sit-in sessions from students across different colleges and departments.

📅

Reservation Management

Manage laboratory reservations, schedules, and available computer workstations efficiently.

🏆

Leaderboard System

Recognize active and responsible students through session evaluations, participation, and laboratory activities.
